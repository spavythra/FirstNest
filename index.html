Buyer checklist (Finland nuances)

  • Check housing company debt share and planned putkiremontti impact window.
  • Review hoitovastike, water billing model, and heating inclusion details.
  • Confirm energy class (E-luku) and winter electricity risk buffer.
  • Verify owned vs leased land and lease escalation clauses.
  • Validate transfer tax rules and first-home buyer eligibility status.
  • Read latest isannoitsijantodistus before final negotiation.
  • Compare district health score and crime trend with resident report count.
  • Confirm daycare queues, tram/bus coverage, and remote-work broadband reliability.
