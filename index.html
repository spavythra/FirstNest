Step sequence

  1. 1Set comfort budget and monthly cost ceiling
  2. 2Get preliminary mortgage promise from at least two lenders
  3. 3Shortlist areas by commute, schools, and service access
  4. 4Attend viewings and inspect housing company records
  5. 5Make offer with condition and financing clauses
  6. 6Sign deed, register ownership, and activate insurance

Buyer checklist (Finland nuances)

  • Check housing company debt share and upcoming renovation schedule (putkiremontti, facade, roof).
  • Review hoitovastike and whether heating/water is included or metered separately.
  • Confirm energy class (E-luku) and estimate winter energy costs.
  • Verify land ownership model (owned plot vs leased municipal/private land).
  • Calculate transfer tax eligibility and first-home buyer exemptions if applicable.
  • Request and read the latest isannoitsijantodistus before final offer.
  • Review resident-reported crime and wellness trends for day and night periods.
  • Validate broadband, daycare queue status, and local public transport reliability.
